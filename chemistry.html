Chemistry reading

A reading log and companion resources for university-level chemistry.

Chemical Structure and Reactivity

James Keeler and Peter Wothers
An Integrated Approach, 2nd edition (Oxford University Press, 2013)
ISBN 9780199604135

This book connects molecular structure, electron behaviour, bonding, thermodynamics and chemical reactions rather than treating each as an isolated subject.

Book information at Oxford University Press

Reading progress

Chapter 1 — Molecules and molecular structures: an overview
Covered in guided discussion: Lewis structures, formal charge, resonance, oxidation numbers, VSEPR, X-ray diffraction, bonding, intermolecular forces, solids and the ideal gas.

Next: Chapter 2 — Electrons in atoms
Quantum mechanics, wavefunctions, orbitals, electron configurations, penetration and shielding, and ionization energies.

Chapters 3 and 4 continue with molecular symmetry and molecular orbital theory.

Questions to investigate

  • Why is water bent whereas carbon dioxide is linear?
  • Why do resonance structures represent one delocalized molecule, not molecules switching between drawings?
  • Why is molecular oxygen attracted to a magnetic field?
  • How can quantum mechanics explain the octet rule, and where does it fail?
